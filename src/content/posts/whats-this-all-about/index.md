If you've somehow found your way here - welcome. I'm Harrison. I'm a marketer, mostly working in the space where data, technology, and creativity all have to get along.

Over the past several years, I've focused on building the systems, structures and frameworks that help marketing teams work smarter: cleaner data, more meaningful customer journeys, and technology that enables ideas rather than complicating them. The kind of solution that works not just because it's technically sound, but because it's thoughtful and purposeful.

This site is a place to share some of that thinking. These ideas shouldn't sit hidden in a folder somewhere - they should be out in the open, where they can be tested, challenged, improved, or spark something new for someone else. Writing forces clarity, and it gives me a reason to document approaches and patterns that would otherwise stay buried in slide decks and solution architecture diagrams.

## what you can expect here

This isn't a personal journal, and it's not a set of polished case studies (although there might be some of those). It's a working space - a home for ideas in progress. Over time, you'll find:

- Practical perspectives on marketing technology and data design
- Approaches to marketing automation and lifecycle strategy
- Tools or frameworks I build along the way in Marketo, GA4, GTM, Google Cloud, or any other tool of the moment
- General musings on advertising media & creative strategy
- Thoughts on emerging platforms and the shifting role of MarTech
- Reflections on how creative thinking and technical architecture can inform each other - the thinking behind those "there has to be a better way to do this" moments

## who this is for

Anyone who works in marketing, ops, analytics, or adjacent roles. Or honestly, anyone who enjoys solving problems with a mix of logic and imagination.

If I'm doing this right, the posts should feel useful rather than indulgent - the kind of content I wish I'd stumbled across earlier in my career.
