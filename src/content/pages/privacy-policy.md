## about this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is handled in connection with:

- **harrisonjennings.au** and any subdomains operated or controlled by the Site operator (collectively, the **Site**). Different parts of the Site may use different hosting, security and analytics providers as described in this policy;
- booking requests made through the Site;
- email communications and enquiries; and
- preliminary enquiries concerning consulting services.

The Site operator is committed to handling personal information responsibly and transparently. Where the _Privacy Act 1988_ (Cth) applies to an activity, personal information will be handled in accordance with that Act and the Australian Privacy Principles.

“Personal information” means information or an opinion about an identified individual, or an individual who is reasonably identifiable.

## personal information collected

The types of personal information collected or received may include:

- a name, email address and other contact details voluntarily provided;
- information contained in emails or other communications;
- booking details submitted through the Cal.com booking service, including a name, email address, time zone, selected meeting time and responses to booking questions;
- information concerning a consulting enquiry or proposed engagement;
- IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, referral information, pages viewed and timestamps;
- website interaction and analytics events;
- cookie and consent preferences; and
- technical, diagnostic and security information associated with use of the Site.

The Site does not collect payment-card, bank-account or government identity-document information.

The Site operator does not intentionally request sensitive information through the Site. Sensitive information includes information about health, racial or ethnic origin, political opinions, religious beliefs, sexual orientation and criminal history. Visitors should avoid providing sensitive information unless it is reasonably necessary for a particular communication or engagement.

## how information is collected

Personal information may be collected:

- directly when a visitor sends an email or other communication;
- when a visitor chooses to load or use the Cal.com booking calendar;
- automatically through the Site’s hosting, content-delivery and security systems;
- through analytics and consent-management technologies in accordance with the visitor’s choices, including limited measurements without cookies where applicable; and
- through a service provider acting on behalf of the Site operator.

The Site can generally be browsed without providing a name or other direct identifier. A visitor may use a pseudonym when making an enquiry where practicable. Sufficient information may nevertheless be required to respond to an enquiry, arrange a meeting or provide a requested service.

## purposes for handling information

Personal information may be collected, used and disclosed for the following purposes:

- operating, maintaining and securing the Site;
- detecting, preventing and investigating malicious or unauthorised activity;
- responding to emails, enquiries and other communications;
- arranging meetings and responding to consulting enquiries;
- administering a consulting relationship where one is established;
- measuring Site performance and usage where analytics consent has been provided;
- diagnosing technical problems and improving the Site;
- maintaining appropriate business and security records;
- obtaining professional advice;
- complying with applicable laws and lawful requests; and
- exercising or defending legal rights.

Personal information will not be used for an unrelated purpose unless that use is permitted by law or appropriate consent has been obtained.

## cookies and similar technologies

The Site uses cookies and similar browser technologies for limited purposes.

### necessary technologies

Necessary technologies may be used to:

- operate and secure the Site;
- detect bots, abuse and malicious traffic;
- remember cookie-consent preferences; and
- provide functionality specifically requested by a visitor.

These technologies may operate without optional analytics or marketing consent where they are reasonably necessary for the requested functionality or the security of the Site.

::cookie-table{category="necessary"}

### analytics

The primary WordPress website uses Google Analytics and related measurement services to understand matters such as visitor numbers, pages viewed and general interaction with the website.

The Site uses Advanced Google Consent Mode. If optional consent is refused, Google may receive limited measurements without cookies for aggregated measurement and modelling. Analytics or advertising cookies are not stored or accessed unless the visitor grants the corresponding consent.

When Analytics consent is granted, analytics information may include online identifiers, page and event information, referral information, approximate location, and browser and device characteristics.

Google Signals is enabled in Google Analytics. Where a visitor grants Advertising and Targeting consent, Google Signals may provide aggregated cross-device, demographic and interest reporting for eligible Google users. The Site operator does not receive information that directly identifies individual Google Account users.

Analytics and advertising consent can be granted, refused or withdrawn at any time using the Site’s cookie settings. Visitors may also use Google’s advertising controls and the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

::cookie-table{category="analytics"}

### advertising and remarketing

The Site does not currently run Google Ads campaigns, use Meta Pixel or conduct remarketing or personalised-advertising campaigns.

Google Analytics advertising features may use advertising cookies or identifiers where Advertising and Targeting consent has been granted. These features are currently used only for aggregated Analytics reporting, including Google Signals, rather than to deliver advertising campaigns.

If advertising or remarketing through Google, Meta or another provider is introduced, this policy and the Site’s consent controls will be updated before those technologies are activated.

## cal.com booking calendar

The Site includes an optional booking calendar provided by Cal.com.

The Cal.com calendar does not load until a visitor deliberately selects the button to load it. Loading the calendar connects the visitor’s browser to Cal.com. Cal.com may receive technical information including the visitor’s IP address, browser and device information, referral information and timestamps. Cal.com or its infrastructure providers may also store information required for security and user preferences.

When a visitor chooses to load the calendar, Cal.com and Cloudflare infrastructure supporting the calendar may set strictly necessary security and session cookies, including cookies used for bot detection, authentication routing and cross-site request-forgery protection. These cookies are classified as necessary because they protect and provide the booking functionality specifically requested by the visitor. They are not used by the Site operator for Site analytics or advertising.

If a visitor makes a booking, Cal.com processes the contact and booking information submitted through the calendar in order to provide the scheduling service. This can include the visitor’s name, email address, selected meeting time, time zone and responses to booking questions.

Cal.com is based in the United States, and booking information may be processed in the United States or other locations used by Cal.com and its service providers.

A visitor who does not wish to connect to Cal.com can choose not to load the calendar and may instead use another contact method published on the Site.

Further information is available in the [Cal.com Privacy Policy](https://cal.com/privacy).

## service providers and disclosures

Personal information may be handled by service providers that assist with operating, securing and communicating through the Site. Current providers include:

- **VentraIP**, for Australian website hosting;
- **Cloudflare**, for DNS, content delivery, traffic management and website security;
- **Google**, including Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager and Google Cloud services used in the Site’s measurement architecture;
- **Vercel**, for hosting and cookieless Web Analytics associated with selected project or demonstration sites;
- **GitHub**, for static GitHub Pages hosting associated with selected project, demonstration or reference sites;
- **Cal.com**, for optional appointment booking; and
- **Apple/iCloud**, for email communications associated with the Site’s domain.

These providers may receive or process information reasonably necessary to provide the relevant service, subject to their respective terms, privacy practices and processing arrangements.

Personal information may also be disclosed:

- to professional advisers where reasonably necessary;
- where required or authorised by law;
- in response to a valid request from a court, regulator or law-enforcement authority;
- where reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, abuse or a security incident; or
- with the affected individual’s consent.

Personal information is not sold or rented.

## overseas processing

Some service providers operate internationally. Personal information may consequently be processed in the United States and other countries in which Cloudflare, Google, Cal.com, Apple, Vercel, GitHub or their service providers operate.

Privacy and data-protection laws in those countries may differ from Australian law. Where required by applicable law, reasonable steps will be taken in connection with the selection and use of service providers and the handling of personal information outside Australia.

Use of an international provider does not necessarily mean that all information is stored permanently outside Australia. Data locations can vary according to the provider, service, routing and configuration involved.

## retention and deletion

Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, including to:

- respond to an enquiry or communication;
- administer a booking or consulting engagement;
- maintain appropriate business, security and technical records;
- resolve disputes; and
- comply with applicable legal, accounting or record-keeping obligations.

When personal information is no longer reasonably required, reasonable steps will be taken to delete or de-identify it where practicable and where continued retention is not required by law.

Information retained by a service provider may be subject to that provider’s retention and backup processes.

## security and data breaches

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect personal information from loss, misuse, interference, unauthorised access, modification and disclosure.

Measures used in connection with the Site include encrypted connections, access controls, multifactor authentication, website security controls, traffic filtering, software maintenance and backups.

No website, transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. If a suspected data breach occurs, the incident will be investigated and appropriate containment, remediation and notification steps will be taken. The Office of the Australian Information Commissioner and affected individuals will be notified where required by applicable law.

## direct marketing

Contact information will not be used to send direct marketing unless:

- the recipient has consented;
- the recipient would reasonably expect that use and it is permitted by law; or
- the communication is otherwise permitted by law.

Any direct marketing communication will provide a straightforward way to opt out. An opt-out request will be actioned as soon as practicable and within any period required by law, without charge.

Information submitted solely to arrange a meeting or make a general enquiry will not automatically be added to a marketing list.

## access and correction

A person may request access to personal information held about them or ask for that information to be corrected.

Requests should be sent to **[privacy@harrisonjennings.au](mailto:privacy@harrisonjennings.au)** and should include enough information to identify the relevant records. Reasonable identity verification may be required before personal information is disclosed or corrected.

A request will ordinarily be addressed within a reasonable period. If access or correction is refused, written reasons will be provided where required or appropriate.

A copy of this policy can also be requested in an alternative reasonably available format.

Personal information collected through the Site is not used to make automated decisions that could reasonably be expected to significantly affect an individual’s rights or interests.

This policy may be updated when the Site’s information-handling practices, service providers or applicable legal requirements change.

The current version will be published on the Site with the date of the latest update. Material changes affecting how personal information is handled will be accompanied by appropriate notice or renewed consent where required.
