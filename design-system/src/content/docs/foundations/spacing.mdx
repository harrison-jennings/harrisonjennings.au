## rules

- **Use a step, not a number.** Gaps and padding come from the scale; a value between two steps is a sign the layout wants rethinking.
- **Sections are 56px apart in a page,** and the home page's sections 96px (72px on phones).
- **Gutters:** 72px on wide screens, 16px on phones.
- **Tight inside, loose between.** Things that belong together sit closer than the things around them.
