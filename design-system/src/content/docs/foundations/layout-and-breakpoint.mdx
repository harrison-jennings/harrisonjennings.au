## the column

Every page's content lines up to `container`, the nav pill's maximum width. Content starts 22px in from the pill's left edge, under the logo, and ends 10px in from its right edge, under the nav's button. The `.column` class in `global.css` does this for you.

- **Gutters:** 72px either side on wide screens, 16px on phones.
- **Reading width:** post text runs to 680px; lines longer than that get hard to follow.

## the breakpoint

There's one: **760px**. Above it is the desktop layout; at 760px and below, the phone layout.

- **Phone type:** the post title drops to 32px, the hero to 44px, section titles to 26px.
- **Phone layouts:** rows of cards swipe sideways, with the next card peeking in; grids become one column; the nav becomes the menu button.
- **Nothing scrolls sideways at 390px.** Long values (addresses, code, colours) wrap or scroll inside their own box, never the page.
