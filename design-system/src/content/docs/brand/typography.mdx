## rules

- **Mono stays short.** Martian Mono is wide. Past two lines it gets hard to read, which is why post titles are in Schibsted.
- **Track display type tight.** -0.05em, or -0.04em at 20px and below. Untracked, the mono looks like a terminal.
- **Give wrapped display lines room.** 1.12 at 46–50px and 1.1 at 34px. Any tighter and descenders hit the hero's dots.
- **Few weights.** Schibsted at 400 to read and 600 for titles. Martian Mono at 500 for display, 400 for code and hostnames. No bold body copy.
- **Muted is for meta.** Dates, captions and the dek are in `muted`; everything you read is in `text`.
