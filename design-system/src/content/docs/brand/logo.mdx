## construction

<LogoConstruction>

- **One line.** Stem, shoulder and leg are a single stroke with round caps and joins, on a 100-unit square.
- **The dot sits at the foot.** It's centred under the leg, and its lowest point lines up with the bottom of the stem.
- **Line in text, dot in accent.** Both follow the theme. Nothing else is ever recoloured.
- **Clear space: two-thirds of the mark's height.** The tightest the site goes is the compact nav: 16px around the 24px mark. Everywhere else it has more.

</LogoConstruction>

## sizes

The line gets heavier as the mark gets smaller, so it reads the same at every size. The component picks the weight from the size you give it. The compact nav is the one place CSS resizes the mark: it shrinks the nav's own drawing, so it keeps the nav's weight. Nothing shows the mark below 24px; below that, use the tile.
