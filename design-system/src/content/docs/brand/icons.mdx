## the set

The icons the `Icon` component draws, at its default 20px.

<IconGrid />

A few components draw their own small icons beside their words: the theme toggle's sun and moon, the copy button, the arrows on swipe rows and the image viewer's controls. They follow the same rules.

## rules

- **One ink.** Icons are drawn in `currentColor`, so they take `text` at rest and `link` on hover with everything else.
- **Line icons are 2px, with round caps and joins.** Brand marks (GitHub, LinkedIn) keep their own filled shapes.
- **A name for every icon on its own.** An icon-only button has an accessible name ("Menu", "Close menu"); an icon beside words is hidden from screen readers.
- **44px to tap.** An icon button's target is `target` (44px) even when the icon is 20px, and 48px in the phone menu.
- **No icon for decoration.** If the words say it, the icon doesn't need to.
