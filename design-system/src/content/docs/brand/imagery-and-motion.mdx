## the ember field

The brick-to-apricot gradient is atmosphere only: the hero field, social share images and the 404 page. Never on text, the logo, buttons or small UI. It's two radial clouds over `ground`; light mode uses richer cloud values on its own.

```css
  radial-gradient(50% 70% at 98% 92%, var(--cloud-apricot), transparent 70%);
```

</div>

- **Copy on the left, clouds on the right.** Text and the lockup sit in the clear corner, never on the gradient.
- **Screenshots are the other imagery.** Projects show the real product, framed by a hairline at `radius-md`.

## rules

- **Still under reduced motion.** Transitions become instant and loops stop, with nothing lost: the still state is a finished design, not a broken one.
- **Quick and quiet.** Hover changes take 150ms. Nothing moves for longer than it needs to, and nothing moves on its own except the loops above.
- **Movement means something.** The button's wipe follows the pointer, the nav compacts as you scroll, the banner rises into place. No decoration for its own sake.
