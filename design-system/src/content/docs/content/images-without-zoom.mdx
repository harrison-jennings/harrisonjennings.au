## writing them

Add `{nozoom}` straight after the image, with no space. In an `.mdx` post braces are code, so write it as `\{nozoom}`; there's a snippet for each.

## when to use them

<DoDont>
  <DoDontCard verdict="do" say="Small images, icons and logos, where there's nothing more to see." />
  <DoDontCard verdict="don't" say="Screenshots and diagrams. Someone will want to read the detail." />
</DoDont>
