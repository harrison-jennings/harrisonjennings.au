## writing them

`![what it shows](./image.png 'caption')`, with the image saved beside the post. The words in square brackets are its alternative text, for anyone who can't see it; the caption, in quotes, is optional and shows underneath. Images keep their own shape: landscape and square ones fill the column, and portrait ones are capped at 560px tall and centred. In VS Code, the media dashboard inserts them: pick the image, then **image** or **image with caption**.
