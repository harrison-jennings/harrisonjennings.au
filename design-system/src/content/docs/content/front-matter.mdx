## what the build checks

- **Lengths.** A description over 180 characters, a search title over 65 or a search description over 155 stops the build, with the post's name.
- **Links between files.** A topic that isn't in topics.yaml, or a related post that doesn't exist, fails the build rather than leaving a dead link.
- **Dates and images.** A date that isn't a date, or a cover that isn't beside the post, fails too.
- **What it works out for you.** Reading time (prose words ÷ 230, rounded up), "on this page" from the headings, and "keep reading".
