## writing one

Three hyphens on a line of their own, with a blank line either side. They draw three brick dots, centred, not a rule.

## when to use them

<DoDont>
  <DoDontCard verdict="do" say="Between two parts of a story, where a heading would be too formal." />
  <DoDontCard verdict="don't" say="Before every heading. A heading already starts a new part." />
</DoDont>
