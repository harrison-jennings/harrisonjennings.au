## writing one

Two parts, matched by number: the marker `[^1]` straight after the word or punctuation, and the note `[^1]: …` on a line of its own anywhere in the post (usually the end). There's a snippet for each. The notes are collected at the end of the post, numbered in order, each linking back to its marker.
