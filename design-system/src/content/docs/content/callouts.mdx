## the three kinds

<DoDont cols={3}>
  <DoDontCard name=":::note" say="Labelled “note”, in grey. Background or context the reader can skip without missing the point." />
  <DoDontCard name=":::tip" say="Labelled “tip”, in brick. A shortcut, or a better way to do what the text describes." />
  <DoDontCard name=":::warning" say="Labelled “heads up”, with a brick edge. Something that can go wrong, and what it costs." />
</DoDont>

The label is written for you: start the block with the kind's name and close it with `:::` on a line of its own. Anything Markdown can do works inside, including links, inline code and lists. A colon inside a sentence is just text, so "4.53:1" or "10:30" stay as written.
