## the three levels

<DoDont cols={3}>
  <DoDontCard name="## section" say="heading-2, 26px. Listed in “on this page”." />
  <DoDontCard name="### sub-section" say="heading-3, 20px. Listed in “on this page”, indented." />
  <DoDontCard name="#### minor heading" say="heading-4, 17px. Not listed." />
</DoDont>

The post's title, from its front matter, is the page's only h1, so a post starts at `##`. Three levels under the title is the ceiling: `#####` and `######` render as `####`, so a deeper heading never looks like a bug. "on this page" shows once a post has three or more sections.

## when to use them

<DoDont>
  <DoDontCard verdict="do" say="Lowercase, and say what the section is about. Readers skim the list before they read.">

  <h2>what a test send can't see</h2>
</div>
  </DoDontCard>
  <DoDontCard verdict="don't" say="Title case, or a heading that only makes sense after reading the section.">

</div>
  </DoDontCard>
</DoDont>
