## in a post

- **Save images beside the post,** in its folder, and link them as `./image.png`. Front Matter's media tab opens in that folder.
- **Diagrams as SVG where you can,** so they stay sharp at any zoom. Otherwise a PNG at two or three times its shown size: the page loads a smaller one, and the image viewer loads the largest only when it opens.
- **Any shape.** Landscape and square images fill the column; portrait ones are capped at 560px tall (480px on phones) and centred, so they never fill the screen.
- **Images side by side** should share a size and shape, so the row lines up.
- **Alternative text** says what the image shows; the caption, if any, says what to look at.

## a cover

`cover` is an image beside the post, shown with it; always add `coverAlt` with it, saying what it shows.

## a project

`image` in `src/content/projects.yaml` is a screenshot in `src/content/projects/`. It's shown at 16:9 and cropped from the top left, so keep the important part there. It's shown still, never as a link.

## a share image

A post's share image is made at build from its title, topic and date. To use a picture instead, set `socialImage` to an image beside the post. It's cropped to 1200 × 630, so keep the subject near the middle.

## a video poster

A YouTube poster is a still frame saved beside the post, at 16:9, imported at the top of the post.
