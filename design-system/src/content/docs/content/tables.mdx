## writing them

A row of headings, a row of hyphens, then a row per item, with `|` between columns. Right-align a column with `---:` and its numbers are set in Martian Mono, lined up. The snippet makes a three-column table with its last column right-aligned. The columns don't need to line up in the Markdown, but the formatter lines them up on save.
