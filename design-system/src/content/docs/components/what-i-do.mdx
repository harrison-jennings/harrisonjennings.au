## how it behaves

- **Three in a row,** sharing their rows (CSS subgrid), so every tools line starts level however long the paragraphs above run.
- **The tools:** Martian Mono 12px in `muted`, each after a small brick dot and never split across lines; a wrapped line still starts with a dot.
- **Phones:** cards 84% wide with the next one peeking in, and ‹ › arrows in the section's head that fade at the ends.
- **Its words** are in `src/content/services.yaml`: a name, a description of up to 200 characters, and exactly three tools each.

## props

None. Its words come from `src/content/services.yaml`.

## accessibility

- **A heading per service,** under the section's heading, so it can be skimmed.
- **The arrows** are labelled buttons, and the row can also be scrolled by touch or keyboard.
