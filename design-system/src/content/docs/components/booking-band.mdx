## how it behaves

- **The band:** "ready to jump in? book a call" with the brick stop, a note, the button, and "or say hello on linkedin".
- **The button** opens the calendar inside the band and becomes "hide availability". Cal.com loads the first time it opens, not before, so nothing from Cal.com runs until someone asks for it.
- **While it loads,** the logo's dot bounces, with words beside it saying what's loading.
- **Every "book a call"** on the site leads here.
- **Phones:** the band stacks, the button goes full width, and the LinkedIn line centres under it.

## props

None. Its words come from `src/content/home.yaml`.

## accessibility

- **The button says what it controls** (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`), and its label changes with the state.
- **The loader has words,** not just the bouncing dot.
- **Reduced motion:** the dot is still while the calendar loads.
