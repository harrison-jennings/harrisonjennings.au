## how it behaves

- **Automatic in posts:** every Markdown image can be opened, with the expand icon in its corner on hover (always shown on phones). An image opts out with `{nozoom}` after it.
- **Zoom** from fit to 400%: the scroll wheel, + and −, a click or tap on the image (fit and 200% at that spot), or a pinch. 0 or the fit button resets.
- **Move** a zoomed image by dragging; it stops at the image's edges.
- **Several images** in a post are one set: ‹ › at the sides on desktop, at the ends of the control bar on phones, arrow keys, and swipes.
- **Close** with Esc, the close button, a click on the empty backdrop, or a swipe down on phones. Focus goes back to the image that opened it.

## images for it

Export diagrams as SVG where you can, so they stay sharp at any zoom; otherwise a PNG at two or three times its shown size. The page loads the smaller image, and the large one only when the viewer opens.

## accessibility

- **A modal dialog** named after the image. Focus moves to close on open and stays inside while it's open; every control is a labelled button.
- **The zoom level is announced** politely as it changes.
- **Reduced motion:** zoom and moving jump instead of easing.
