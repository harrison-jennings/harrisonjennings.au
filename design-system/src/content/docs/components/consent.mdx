## how it behaves

- **The banner:** a raised card 24px from the bottom-right corner on desktop, clear of the hero's headline and the nav; on phones it spans the bottom of the screen. "cookies." with the brick stop, one paragraph, then three choices of equal weight: "accept all", "reject non-essential", "manage preferences".
- **The preferences:** a 720px dialog with a panel per category (essential, analytics, advertising). Each opens to say what it's for and list its cookies. "save choices" is the one primary button; on phones the dialog fills the screen.
- **Switches** for analytics and advertising; essential is "always on".
- **Nothing optional runs** until it's turned on. The choice is kept by CookieConsent and recorded in the site's consent log, as the privacy policy describes.
- **The banner never blocks the page:** it's a labelled region, not a modal.
- **Its words** are in `src/content/consent.yaml`.

## props

None. Its words come from `src/content/consent.yaml`.

## accessibility

- **Equal choices:** rejecting is as easy as accepting, one button each, the same size.
- **The banner is a region, not a modal,** so the page stays usable behind it; the preferences are a real dialog, with focus moving in and back.
- **Switches are real switches** (`role="switch"`, `aria-checked`), named by their category, with 44px hit areas.
- **Reduced motion:** the banner and dialog appear without sliding.
