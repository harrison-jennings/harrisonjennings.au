## how it behaves

- **Where it sits:** "blog / \{main topic\}", linked back.
- **The title** in `title-xl`, Schibsted rather than Martian Mono because titles run past two lines, with balanced wrapping; 32px on phones.
- **The dek** is the post's `description`, in `muted`: the same words as search results show, unless the post sets its own for search.
- **The date and reading time,** written out ("13 August 2026, 6 min read"), then every topic as a tag.
- **A cover image,** if the post has one.
