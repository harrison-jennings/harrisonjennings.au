## how it behaves

- **Who:** the lockup at 28px with the name at 20px, which draws when the footer comes into view and again on hover, and the tagline.
- **Where to go:** blog, projects and "book a call".
- **Where else:** GitHub and LinkedIn as an icon and a word, then privacy, terms and "cookie settings", 16px apart, each turning `link` on hover.
- **The panel:** `surface`, `radius-lg`, three columns (1.4 / 1 / 1); one column on phones.

## props

None. Its links come from `src/consts.ts`, as the nav's do.

## accessibility

- **"cookie settings" on every page,** so a choice can always be changed (it reopens the consent preferences).
- **GitHub and LinkedIn are words as well as icons,** and say they open in a new tab.
- **A `contentinfo` landmark,** as the page's one `<footer>`.
