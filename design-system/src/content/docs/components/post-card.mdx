## how it behaves

- **The date,** written out, in `muted`.
- **The title** is the link. On hover its underline grows in from the left (240ms) and it turns `link`.
- **The description** is the post's own `description` front matter, the same text search engines see, kept short by the schema rather than cut off.
- **One tag:** the post's first topic, linked once that topic has a page.
- **`PostCardView`** is the card's look on its own, filled by hand: the voice page uses it for its before and after.

## accessibility

- **One link per card,** the title, so a screen reader's list of links isn't doubled.
- **An `<article>`** with a heading, so cards can be skimmed by heading.
- **Reduced motion:** the underline appears at once.
