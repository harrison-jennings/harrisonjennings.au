## how they're made

- **At build time,** from each page's title, topic, date and summary: Satori lays the card out in the brand fonts, and sharp turns it into a PNG at `/og/<path>.png`.
- **Every card:** `ground`, the ember clouds top right (stronger on home and project cards, softer behind the wordier post and page cards), the lockup bottom left and a detail bottom right in Martian Mono.
- **Dark only,** because share previews have no light mode.
- **A post's own image:** `socialImage` in its front matter replaces its card, cropped to 1200 × 630.

## accessibility

- **The page's own title and description** go with the card, so the image never has to carry the meaning.
- **Text on the card** is the same colours as the site, at the same contrast.
