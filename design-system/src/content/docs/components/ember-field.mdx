## how it behaves

- **The dots** rest in `field-dot` and drift gently. The pointer pushes them aside and lights them in `link`, sending a ring out every 1.5s; when nobody's pointing, a slow wanderer sends one every 2.8s. A click or tap sends one too.
- **The headline's brick stop** pulses two small rings that stay clear of the letters.
- **The clouds** behind it are the ember clouds, at 70% opacity: brick high on the right, apricot low in the corner.
- **It pauses** when it's off screen, and keeps the same pace on 60 Hz and 120 Hz screens.

## where it goes

The home page's first screen only. The 404 page has the clouds without the dots, and share images have the clouds as a still picture.

## accessibility

- **Decoration only:** the canvas is hidden from screen readers, and nothing depends on it.
- **Reduced motion:** it draws once, still, with a soft lit patch top right, redrawn now and then so a theme change shows.
- **Contrast:** the headline and buttons sit on the left, clear of the clouds, at their usual contrast.
