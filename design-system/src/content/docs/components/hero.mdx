## how it behaves

- **Fills the first screen,** between 640px and 1080px tall (560px at least on phones). The nav floats over it.
- **The headline** is `display-hero`, fluid from 40px to 96px (44px on phones), nudged so it lines up under the logo, and ends in the brick stop, which pulses two small rings.
- **The introduction:** two sentences on separate lines, the first in `text`, the second in `muted`.
- **Actions** at the column's right edge: "read the blog", then a large "book a call". On phones they stack: the button full width, the link centred under it.
- **The scroll cue** sits bottom left and fades over the first 160px of scroll.
- **Its words** are in `src/content/home.yaml`, so they change without touching the component.

## props

None. Its words come from `src/content/home.yaml`.

## accessibility

- **One `<h1>`,** the headline, labelling the section. The stop and the cue are hidden from screen readers.
- **The field is decoration;** nothing depends on it.
- **Reduced motion:** the field draws once, still; no rings; the cue doesn't move.
