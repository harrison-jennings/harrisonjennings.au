## how it behaves

- **Where:** posts only, fixed to the column's right edge, so on desktop it sits under "on this page" and on phones in the corner.
- **When it shows:** once you've scrolled a screen down. It fades in and out, and stays hidden while the cookie banner is up, as they share the corner.
- **The ring** fills from the start of the post's text to its end, so it's full when you've read it, before the author panel and "keep reading".
- **Activating it** scrolls to the top: smoothly, or at once under reduced motion.
- **Without JavaScript** it never shows.

## props

None.

## accessibility

- **A button named "back to top",** 44px, with the site's focus ring.
- **Focus moves to the post's title,** so the next Tab carries on from the top, not from the corner.
- **Hidden means hidden:** until it shows, it's out of the tab order and unseen by screen readers.
