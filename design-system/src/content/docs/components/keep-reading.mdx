## how it behaves

- **Which three** (`keepReading` in `src/lib/blog.ts`): a post's hand-picked `related` posts if it names any; otherwise the posts sharing the most topics with it, then the latest.
- **Cards** are the post card, three across; on phones a swipe row with arrows.
